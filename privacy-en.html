2. Data processed

Optional data includes profile details, training avatar, workout records, derived progress values and app settings. G04Fit does not create medical diagnoses.

3. Consent and local storage

G04Fit stores profile, workout and settings data locally in the browser or app. Each category has separate consent. Without consent, that data exists only in memory until the app closes. Export, import, withdrawal and deletion are available in the app.

4. Push notifications

Only when enabled, a random device ID, Web Push subscription, training days, reminder time, timezone and language are sent to the configured G04Fit Cloudflare push service. Name, photo, profile and workout history are not sent. The schedule is deleted when consent is withdrawn.

5. Rights and deletion

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and withdraw consent. All data can be deleted directly in G04Fit. Contact the controller above for enquiries.

6. No medical advice

G04Fit is not a medical device and does not replace medical or physiotherapy advice.
