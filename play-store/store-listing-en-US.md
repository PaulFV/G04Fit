G04Fit

## Short description (≤ 80 characters)

Personal workout planner with weekly plans, progress tracking and motivation.

## Full description

G04Fit helps you build a consistent strength-training routine for chest, back, abs,
biceps, triceps and shoulders. Create a weekly plan, follow your journey, track your
progress and get practical suggestions from the on-device coach.

Highlights:

* Weekly workout plans and flexible training days
* Exercise library with form tips and muscle focus
* Progress, records, streaks and volume tracking
* Optional motivational reminders on your training days
* English by default, with German available in Profile
* Privacy-first storage with separate consent controls
* Export, import and complete deletion of your data

G04Fit is not a medical product and does not replace professional medical advice.
